What is an Anagram?

An anagram is a word or phrase formed by rearranging the letters of a different word or phrase, using all the original letters exactly once. For example:

  • listen → silent, enlist, tinsel, inlets
  • earth → heart, hater, rathe
  • astronomer → moon starer, no more stars
  • dormitory → dirty room

Anagrams have been used throughout history in literature, cryptography, science, and entertainment.
